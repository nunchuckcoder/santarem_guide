# Santarém Guide

**Santarém Guide** helps you explore the city through 37 places organised in
trails, with descriptions in Portuguese and English. The catalogue, history
and legends are available without an internet connection; the map needs one.

> **Status:** version 1.0 is being prepared for closed testing on Google Play.

## Features

- **Five trails:** Ancient and Medieval Santarém, Gothic,
  Manueline/Renaissance, Mannerism/Baroque and Points of Interest.
- **Explore:** search, filters, favourites and sorting by trail, name or
  distance when location is available.
- **Map:** places, filters and previews. It needs internet access and a build
  with a working Google Maps key.
- **AR view:** nearby places over the camera image, with orientation and
  distance. It depends on device sensors and precise location.
- **Photographs:** capture through your device's camera app, gallery
  publication and recovery of pending publications.
- **History and legends:** editorial text and illustrations included in the app.
- **Preferences:** Portuguese or English, and light, dark or automatic theme.

The Church of the Holy Miracle and Portas do Sol Garden / Viewpoint currently
use the default place image until verified photographs are available.

## Privacy

There are no online accounts, advertisements or analytics implemented by the
app. Favourites and preferences stay on the device. Location and camera
access are requested when the corresponding features are used. The map uses
Google Maps, whose SDK may process technical data; gallery photos may retain
camera EXIF metadata, including GPS coordinates.

Read the [Privacy Policy](docs/en/index.md), the
[Portuguese version](docs/index.md), [Support](docs/support/index.md) and
[data deletion instructions](docs/delete-data/index.md).

## Requirements

- Android 8.0 or later.
- Internet for the map; editorial content works offline.
- Location for distances and the map; precise location and camera for AR.
- A camera app to take photographs; Android 8/9 may require storage permission
  to publish to the gallery.
