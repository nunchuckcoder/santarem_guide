# Privacy Policy — Santarém Guide

[Português](../) · [English](./) · [Support](../support/) · [Delete data](../delete-data/)

**Last updated:** 30 September 2026  
**Controller:** Osvaldo Cipriano (NunchuckCoder)  
**Contact:** [osvaldo@osvaldocipriano.dev](mailto:osvaldo@osvaldocipriano.dev)

This policy explains how the **Santarém Guide** Android app handles data on
the device and which external services it uses.

## 1. Controller and scope

Santarém Guide is developed by Osvaldo Cipriano. Trails, descriptions,
history and legends can be browsed without an account or an internet
connection. The developer does not maintain a user database or receive
favourites, preferences or photographs through a developer-operated server.

## 2. Data stored on the device

The app may locally store favourites, welcome completion, theme and language
preferences, and the records needed to publish photographs. This data
provides the app's features. Santarém Guide has no online accounts.

## 3. Location and camera

The app may request location when you use distances, the map or the augmented
reality (AR) view. AR requires precise location. The app does not request
background location. The camera is used in AR and when you choose to take a
photo. The AR view does not record the camera image.

You can deny or revoke permissions in Android settings; related features
may then be unavailable. Location may be processed by the Google services
used to display the map and obtain the device position, under those services'
applicable policies.

## 4. Google Maps and external actions

The map requires internet access and is provided by the Google Maps SDK for
Android. The SDK may collect IP address, device and request information,
crash metrics, an SDK identifier and map interactions. The app also uses
Google Play services to obtain location. See the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) to learn how
Google handles data.

If you open a walking route, your chosen navigation app receives the
destination. If you share a place, you choose which app receives that place's
text. You initiate these actions.

## 5. Photographs and metadata

> **Important:** your device's camera app may write EXIF metadata into a photo,
> including date, camera information and GPS coordinates if geotagging is
> enabled. Santarém Guide publishes the photo to the gallery with the metadata
> written by the camera. Check your camera settings before taking a photo.

The capture is first saved in the app's private storage and then published
to the gallery in **Pictures/Santarem Guide**. Publishing can continue in the
background and resume after an interruption. If it fails, the private
original remains pending for an automatic or manual retry; it is removed only
after publishing is confirmed. Gallery synchronisation with an external
service depends on your settings and chosen services.

## 6. Advertising, analytics and backups

Santarém Guide contains no advertising or its own analytics tools. Private
files, preferences and databases are excluded by the app's configured
backup and device transfer rules. Photos already published to the gallery
are independent files and follow gallery and system settings.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Favourites and preferences remain on the device until removed, until you
clear app data or until you uninstall the app. Pending captures remain in
private storage until publishing is confirmed or app data is deleted.
Uninstalling the app does not remove photos already in the gallery; delete
them in the gallery. To remove the app's private data, use Android storage
settings or uninstall the app. See the [detailed instructions](../delete-data/).

## 8. Security

Private data is stored in the Android storage assigned to the app. The app's
configuration blocks unencrypted HTTP traffic. The behaviour of external
services and the gallery also depends on their apps and settings.

## 9. Children

Santarém Guide is a visitor guide for the general public and is not
specifically directed at children. It has no accounts or profiles for minors.

## 10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if features or data handling practices change.
The latest revision date appears at the top of the page.
